02 · What this template collects

Nothing. Every form on this site is validated in the browser and no data is transmitted, stored or shared. There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel and no third-party tag on any page.

03 · Cookies and local storage

The site uses session storage for two things only: remembering that the introduction has played, and smoothing the page transition between routes. Both are cleared when the browser session ends. No cookie is set.

04 · Third-party requests

The pages request two Google Fonts families and, where the CDN is reachable, GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis. If the CDN cannot be reached the site falls back to the copies stored in assets/vendor. No other external request is made at runtime.
