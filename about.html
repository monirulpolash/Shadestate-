01 · Our story

We began with one site, and a refusal to rush it.

In 2008 three partners bought a plot in Al Barsha to build three houses, and stop. The third one started everything.

Eighteen years later the method has not changed: a few sites a year, read slowly, carried from feasibility to keys. In Dubai we build around shade and water; in Saudi Arabia, from the ground itself.

We keep a stake in most of what we build, and manage it afterwards. That is how a building is still good in year twenty.
