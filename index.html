01 — Philosophy

We build slowly, so the buildings can last without us.

Shadestate is a family practice of architects, developers and craftsmen working along Portugal's Atlantic edge. Each project begins with the land — its light, its wind, the stone already beneath it.

We keep the portfolio deliberately small: three or four houses a year. That restraint is the whole method. It lets the same hands draw the threshold, the joint and the shadow, and it lets us refuse the things that would date a room in a decade.

Tomás Shadestate

Founding partner, architect RIBA

06 — Craft & Materials

A palette that improves with weather.

We work from a short list of materials that are quarried, felled or cast within a day's drive of each site. They are chosen for how they behave in twenty years, not how they photograph on the day of handover.

  1. 01

    Portuguese limestone

    Cut from quarries in Alcanede and Pérola, laid in deep single courses so the façade reads as mass rather than surface.

  2. 02

    Fumed oak

    Smoked with ammonia rather than stained, so the colour runs through the board and lightens gracefully at the edges.

  3. 03

    Lime plaster

    Hand-applied in three coats. It breathes, it softens the acoustics of a room, and it repairs without a trace.

  4. 04

    Solid bronze

    Handles, reveals and thresholds cast in a Lisbon foundry. Left unlacquered, it darkens with touch.
